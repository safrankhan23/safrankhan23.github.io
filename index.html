About Me

Safran Khan is a PhD Candidate in Civil and Environmental Engineering at Utah State University and a Research Assistant at the Utah Water Research Laboratory. His research explores how artificial intelligence, computer vision, and low cost sensing can improve the way water systems and civil infrastructure are monitored. His current work focuses on camera based hydrologic monitoring, including river stage, surface velocity, and discharge estimation, with an emphasis on deep learning, optical flow, edge computing, and field deployable sensing systems.

Before joining Utah State University, he completed an MS in Civil Engineering at Kyungpook National University in South Korea, where his research focused on deep learning for structural crack detection and computer vision. Across his work, he is interested in moving AI methods beyond controlled experiments and into practical engineering systems that can operate continuously in real environments.
